The studio

Built by a player
who never stopped.

DBK Games was founded by Burak Dabak, a game designer who spent years on the other side of the screen first: writing for Chip, Merlin'in Kazanı and GamerTR, editing at GameHQ, and beta testing for studios like Blizzard and Microsoft.

After years studying game programming and graphic design, he shipped Mesel. SpaceBourne followed and found players around the world. SpaceBourne 2 went further still.
