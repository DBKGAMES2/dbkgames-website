About the game

Create your own novel with your own decisions.

Mesel takes place in the abandoned Gölcük Children's Hospital. All it wants from you is to be discovered.

Every choice you make and every mystery you uncover is added to your novel, so how it reads depends on how you play. Depending on your style, the novel can run up to 100 pages.

There is no fighting or shooting. Intelligence, curiosity and a sharp eye are all you need.

Available on Steam

Languages: English, Turkish. Full English voice-over.

Features

What you'll do

01

Visions from the past

Use flashbacks to reveal hidden memories and add them to your novel.

02

Your novel

Choices and discoveries become pages. A full playthrough can write up to 100 of them.

03

Puzzles

Solve the puzzles waiting in the hospital to reveal more of its mysteries.

04

Real documents

Notes, records and letters left behind, most of them taken from real hospital records.

05

No combat

No bots to kill. You get through it by observing and thinking.

06

One haunting place

A single, detailed setting: an abandoned children's hospital in Gölcük.
